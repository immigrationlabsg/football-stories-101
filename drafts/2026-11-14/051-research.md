## Claims and sources

1. **19 Nov 2005, Real Madrid 0-3 Barcelona at the Santiago Bernabéu (La Liga, matchday 12).**
   - FC Barcelona, "15 years to the day since a famous Clásico" (opened): "19 November 2005 ... Barça won 3-0 away".
     https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/1899174/15-years-to-the-day-since-a-famous-clasico
   - Soccer-Spain match report, First Division Day 12 (opened): "Real Madrid 0-3 Barcelona".
     https://www.soccer-spain.com/ssdocs/reports2005/div1/day12/report8.html
   - Also Planet Football forensic analysis (opened).

2. **The home fans stood and applauded Ronaldinho; it came after his second goal (the team's third, 0-3).**
   - Yahoo Sport, "Iconic Performances: the night Ronaldinho received a standing ovation" (opened): crowd "rose to applaud" after his second goal.
     https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/iconic-performances-night-ronaldinho-received-131500337.html
   - Sky Sports, "Ronnie moved by applause" (opened): Real Madrid fans applauded Ronaldinho after his third goal [Barcelona's third].
     https://www.skysports.com/football/news/2355384/ronnie-moved-by-applause
   - beIN Sports (opened): after the second goal "many stood and applauded the opposing player".
   - FC Barcelona 15-years page also: "standing ovation from the Madrid fans".

3. **Ronaldinho scored twice, Eto'o once; Eto'o scored first ("went ahead early", 14th/15th minute).**
   - FC Barcelona 15-years page: Eto'o 14', Ronaldinho 59', 77'.
   - Soccer-Spain: Eto'o 15', Ronaldinho 59', 77'.
   - Minor disagreement on 14' v 15' (and 58'/59' in one search excerpt): body says "early" and "just before the hour", no minute.

4. **Only one Barcelona player had been applauded at the Bernabéu before: Diego Maradona (1983); 22 years.**
   - Soccer-Spain: first time a Barcelona player received such an accolade at the Bernabéu "since Maradona scored there about 22 years earlier".
   - Yahoo Sport: "the first Barcelona player since the legendary Diego Maradona to receive appreciative applause from the Bernabeu crowd".
   - Also Scroll.in (opened): "Diego Maradona was the only Barcelona player to receive a similar tribute"; Sky: Ronaldinho "didn't know that it had only happened here before with Maradona".
   - 1983 to 2005 = 22 years (arithmetic; Soccer-Spain says "about 22 years").

5. **June 1983, League Cup (Copa de la Liga) final at the Bernabéu: Maradona rounded the goalkeeper, Juan José slid in and hit the post, Maradona scored, the crowd applauded; Barcelona won the cup.**
   - FC Barcelona, "30 years since Maradona stunned the Santiago Bernabéu" (opened): 26 June 1983, League Cup; Maradona went past Agustín [the Madrid goalkeeper]; Juan José crashed into the post; the home crowd stood and applauded; a competition Barcelona went on to win.
     https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/1138728/30-years-since-maradona-stunned-the-santiago-bernabeu
   - Futbolretro, "The day Diego Armando Maradona came out cheered at the Santiago Bernabéu" (opened): 26 June 1983, first leg of the League Cup final at the Bernabéu, 2-2; dribbled goalkeeper Agustín, waited for defender Juan José, scored; the crowd cheered him; Barcelona won the second leg 2-1.
     https://futbolretro.es/diego-armando-maradona-salio-ovacionado-del-bernabeu/?lang=en
   - FC Barcelona "The big read: Maradona, the FC Barcelona years" (opened) also: "Maradona's goal in the Santiago Bernabéu drew applause even from the Real Madrid fans"; Juan José "crashed painfully into the post".
   - Dropped: which leg it was (FCB's big read implies second leg, Futbolretro says first leg) and whether the ball hit the post on its way in (Futbolretro only). Body says only "in a League Cup final".

6. **Luxemburgo's Real Madrid fielded Zidane, Ronaldo, Raúl, Roberto Carlos and Beckham.**
   - Soccer-Spain XI: "Casillas; Michel Salgado, Sergio Ramos, Iván Helguera, Roberto Carlos; Beckham, Pablo García (Baptista 67'), Zidane; Raúl (Guti 58'); Robinho, Ronaldo"; "Luxemburgo is already looking over his shoulder".
   - FC Barcelona, "15 años de un Clásico histórico" (opened): same Madrid XI; Madrid coach Vanderlei Luxemburgo. https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/noticias/1899174/15-anos-de-un-clasico-historico
   - Trivela (opened): "Zidane, Ronaldo, Beckham, Raúl, Roberto Carlos e Casillas estavam em campo", coached by Vanderlei Luxemburgo.
     https://trivela.com.br/espanha/la-liga/ha-15-anos-ronaldinho-vivia-seu-mais-inesquecivel-classico-com-o-barca-ovacionado-pelo-bernabeu/

7. **Frank Rijkaard's Barcelona.** FC Barcelona 15-years page ("under Frank Rijkaard"); Trivela ("Frank Rijkaard podia escalar...").

8. **Messi, 18, in his first Clásico, set up Eto'o's opener.**
   - FC Barcelona 15-years page: "excellent finish" from a pass by "debutant Messi", aged 18, first appearance in the fixture.
   - Goal, "Lionel Messi's El Clasico debut: who were his team-mates?" (opened): his first El Clásico, 19 Nov 2005, 3-0.
     https://www.goal.com/en/lists/lionel-messi-el-clasico-debut-who-were-his-teammates/152te1jwej0hj1tl9tif8848ie
   - Trivela: "um garoto chamado Lionel Messi, então com 18 anos" broke away and Eto'o controlled and scored.
   - messivsronaldo.app (search excerpt): 15' Eto'o goal listed as Messi's first La Liga and first Clásico assist. https://www.messivsronaldo.app/messi-all-assists/real-madrid-19-11-2005/
   - Soccer-Spain: Eto'o "took the ball off Messi" and scored. (Messi born 24 June 1987, so 18 on the day.)

9. **First Ronaldinho goal: from the left, past Sergio Ramos and Iván Helguera, beat Casillas; just before the hour.**
   - Planet Football (opened): went past Sergio Ramos and Ivan Helguera, finished at the near post past Iker Casillas, around the hour mark.
     https://www.planetfootball.com/nostalgia/a-forensic-analysis-of-ronaldinho-earning-an-ovation-at-real-madrid
   - Yahoo Sport: "just before the hour", picked up the ball wide on the left, Sergio Ramos beaten, side-stepped Ivan Helguera.
   - Trivela: "entortar Sergio Ramos e Helguera". FCB: "cutting in from the left".
   - (FCB Spanish page names Salgado and Ramos; all agree on Ramos; Helguera has three sources.)

10. **Second Ronaldinho goal: 77' ("13 minutes left"), from the left, past Ramos, far corner.**
    - Soccer-Spain: 77', "rounded Sergio Ramos and placed a shot into the far corner".
    - Yahoo Sport: got past Sergio Ramos again, finished past Casillas.
    - Planet Football: "slid the ball into Casillas' far corner"; FCB: 77', "again cutting in from the left".

11. **2003: Joan Laporta ran for Barcelona president promising to sign Beckham; Manchester United accepted his offer on condition he won.**
    - Arab News / Reuters, "Beckham's future in balance after Laporta bid", London 12 June 2003 (opened): "United announced on Tuesday they had accepted an undisclosed offer from Joan Laporta"; "Laporta must win the election and then agree personal terms with the player".
      https://www.arabnews.com/node/232796
    - Goal (Laporta to Marca, opened): "United told us that they would sell him to us if we won the [presidential] election".
      https://www.goal.com/en-za/news/barcelona-felt-used-by-beckham-so-signed-ronaldinho-instead/l1ps57srcs171sbpvn1yekdjl
    - Yahoo UK (opened): Laporta "had promised to sign Beckham as part of his election manifesto". https://uk.news.yahoo.com/manchester-united-apos-failure-sell-122600328.html
    - 3Cat (opened): in 2003 Laporta "promet l'arribada de Beckham al club blaugrana". https://www.3cat.cat/3catinfo/la-veritat-del-fitxatge-de-beckham-pel-barca/noticia/3063306/

12. **Laporta won; Beckham signed for Real Madrid instead.**
    - Goal (Laporta): "they used us and in the end he signed for Madrid."
    - 3Cat: Beckham ended up at Real Madrid, not Barça. Yahoo UK: same. (Laporta winning: Arab News conditional + all later accounts of his presidency.)

13. **Barcelona signed Ronaldinho from Paris Saint-Germain that summer (2003).**
    - FC Barcelona, "19 July 2003: Ronaldinho arrives" (opened): paid Paris Saint-Germain "some 25M euros plus 5M in variables".
      https://fcbarcelona.com/en/news/1096193/19-july-2003-ronaldinho-arrives
    - Yahoo UK: Barcelona signed him from PSG in the summer of 2003; Goal (Laporta): "we signed Ronaldinho instead."

14. **Beckham was in Madrid's midfield that night.** Soccer-Spain XI (Beckham in the midfield three); FCB Spanish page XI; Trivela.

15. **"Ronaldinho was not Barcelona's first choice" / "the player Barcelona had wanted".** Follows from claims 11-13: Laporta's own account (Goal, Yahoo UK) that Barcelona chose Ronaldinho after the Beckham deal failed.
