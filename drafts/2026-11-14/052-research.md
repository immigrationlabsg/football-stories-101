# 052 research: Benzema v Salah / Two openers on 10 December that won nothing on the night

Method note: WebFetch opened pages this session. "Opened" means the page was fetched and the words quoted come from it; "excerpt" means a WebSearch result excerpt only. Wikipedia was used only as a pointer. Before scheduling, an editor may wish to reopen the M&G and Fox pages (both opened today).

## Why this pair and this thread
- Kept the suggested pair (Benzema v Salah). Alternative checked: Ronaldo v Kane, 10 Dec 2022 (Qatar 2022), still breaks the one-tournament-a-week rule with 046 Giroud (4 Dec, Qatar 2022). No clearly stronger, fully sourceable 10 December pair was found.
- Thread (not the Puskás Award, which is 049's thread): a paradox. Both men scored the opening goal on 10 December, and neither team won. Then the seasons ran the opposite way: Real lost that night but won the league with 100 points; Salah set a scoring record but Liverpool finished fourth. Five months later the two met in the 2018 Champions League final, where Benzema scored (after a goalkeeper's mistake, like the Clásico goal) and Salah went off injured. Real won 3–1, the same score as the Clásico.
- The Puskás Award is not mentioned at all. 022 (Van Persie v Salah) covered the 10 Nov 2019 header v City and Liverpool's 2019-20 title; nothing here repeats it.
- One-legend rule: Benzema is not in any story within 7 days (`grep "^players:"` 040–047: none). Salah's last feature is 022 (10 Nov), a month before. Cristiano Ronaldo, Alan Shearer and Luis Suárez appear only as passing mentions (old record holders) and are not in `players`. Cristiano Ronaldo may be featured in 050 (Galácticos, 8 Dec): the mention here is passing only, so it should not trigger the rule; the master should check.
- Topical tie-in: UEFA lists Champions League matchday 6 on 8/9 December 2026, including Liverpool v Porto and Arsenal v Real Madrid on 9 December (search excerpt of uefa.com fixture pages). Neither has an honest link to these two moments (no Real v Liverpool or Barcelona/Everton game), so no tie-in is used.
