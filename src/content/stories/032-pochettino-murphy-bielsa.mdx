Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United's head coach is on Sunday, at Anfield.

Here's what I didn't know until this week.

He has never lost his first game at a new club. Espanyol, Southampton, Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea: five club debuts, and not one defeat.

And Anfield is the ground that keeps beating him.

It didn't start that way. In September 2013, his Southampton side won 1-0 at Anfield. Dejan Lovren headed the winner.

At Tottenham, Pochettino built a side that beat Jürgen Klopp's Liverpool 4-1 at Wembley in October 2017. It was his only win over Klopp.

But at Anfield, the wins stopped.

In March 2019, Spurs lost 2-1 there in the 90th minute. Mohamed Salah's header was pushed out by Hugo Lloris, and it went in off Toby Alderweireld.

In October 2019, Harry Kane put Spurs ahead inside the first minute at Anfield. Liverpool still won 2-1.

In between came the 2019 Champions League final in Madrid. Moussa Sissoko was penalised for handball within the first minute, and Liverpool won 2-0.

In January 2024 he went back with Chelsea. "We want to be party poopers," he said. Chelsea lost 4-1.

That makes one win in eight Premier League visits to Anfield.

So on Sunday, something has to give: the record that has never broken, or the ground that never lets him win.

Can Pochettino win at Anfield on Sunday?
