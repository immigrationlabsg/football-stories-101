Marta Vieira da Silva grew up in Dois Riachos, a small town in Alagoas, in the north-east of Brazil.

At 14 she took a bus to Rio de Janeiro for a trial with Vasco da Gama's women's team. The journey took three days.

Vasco took her on.

In 2004 she moved to Umeå IK in Sweden. That spring, aged 18, she scored three goals over the two legs of the UEFA Women's Cup final, an 8–0 aggregate win against Frankfurt.

FIFA voted her the best women's player in the world five years running, from 2006 to 2010, and a sixth time in 2018.

On 18 June 2019, at the Women's World Cup in France, she scored a penalty against Italy. It was her 17th World Cup goal, one more than Miroslav Klose's 16, then the men's record.
