Jay-Jay Okocha was a teenager when he went to Germany in 1990 to visit a friend who played for Borussia Neunkirchen, a third-division club.

He went along to training and joined in. The coach offered him a contract.

He later joined Eintracht Frankfurt. In 1993, against Karlsruhe, he ran at the defence, dribbled past the goalkeeper Oliver Kahn and scored. Frankfurt won 3–1. Viewers of the German TV programme Sportschau voted it the Goal of the Year.

In 1996 he won Olympic gold with Nigeria. They beat Brazil 4–3 in the semi-final and Argentina 3–2 in the final, and became the first African team to win the Olympic football tournament.

In 1998 Paris Saint-Germain signed him from Fenerbahçe, and he became the most expensive African player of the time.

In 2002 he joined Bolton Wanderers and later became captain. Bolton fans sang that he was so good they named him twice.

Here's what stays with me.

He went to Germany to visit a friend.

One training session turned a visit into a career.
