Didier Drogba was five when his parents sent him from Abidjan to France, to live with his uncle Michel Goba, a professional footballer.

He joined Le Mans, then in Ligue 2, but did not sign his first professional contract until he was 21.

On 8 October 2005, Ivory Coast won 3–1 in Sudan and qualified for their first World Cup. In the dressing room, in front of the television cameras, Drogba asked both sides in his country's civil war to put down their weapons.

At Chelsea he became the man for finals. He scored in the FA Cup finals of 2007, 2009, 2010 and 2012, the first player ever to score in four.
