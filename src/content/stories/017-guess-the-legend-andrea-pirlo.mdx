Andrea Pirlo made his Serie A debut for Brescia on 21 May 1995, aged 16. No Brescia player had ever played in the league younger.

He was a number 10 then, the player behind the strikers.

In 2000–01 he went back to Brescia on loan. The number 10 there belonged to Roberto Baggio, his childhood idol. So the coach, Carlo Mazzone, moved Pirlo to the base of midfield, in front of the defence.

It changed his career. At Milan, Carlo Ancelotti used him there too.

In 2011 Milan let him go on a free transfer. Juventus signed him and won the league in each of his four seasons in Turin.

Then came Euro 2012. In the quarter-final shootout against England, he chipped his penalty softly down the middle as Joe Hart dived to one side.

Here's what stays with me.

The best idea of his career came from a coach solving a problem: two players, one position.
