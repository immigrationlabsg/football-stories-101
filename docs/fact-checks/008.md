# Fact-check log: 008 Arrigo Sacchi's Milan / The shoe salesman who taught a team to defend by attacking

Checked 18 October 2026. Method note: page fetching (WebFetch and curl) was blocked by the session's network policy, and BBC, Guardian, Reuters, Telegraph and Independent are excluded from the search tool (a BBC page surfaced once via a short link and is cited only as reported in the search excerpt). Every claim was checked against web-search excerpts from the pages named below, not by opening the full pages. About 30 searches were used. Before scheduling, an editor should open the UEFA "case for greatness" page (Ancelotti quote) and a primary Italian source for the jockey quote.

Living-persons rule: Sacchi, Ancelotti, Gullit, Van Basten and others appear only in documented football facts. In the Marseille walk-off, the Milan director who refused to restart is not named; the text states only the club's refusal and UEFA's official sanction. Alleged eye-gouges, bribery or misconduct were not used.
