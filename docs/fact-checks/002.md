# Fact-check 002: Ferenc Puskás / The exile who scored 156 goals for Real Madrid

Checked 18 October 2026. Method note: WebFetch and curl could not open any page from
this session (network policy blocks the hosts), and BBC, Guardian, Reuters, Telegraph and
Independent are excluded from the search tool. Every claim below was checked against the
text of pages **as returned in web-search result excerpts**, not against the full pages.
The URLs are those pages. Wikipedia appeared in results and was used only as a pointer.
About 20 searches were spent on this story. Anything that could not be supported by two
sources this way was cut or softened. The books and the Times report in the old source
list were not consulted and have been removed from `sources`.
