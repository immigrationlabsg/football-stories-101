# Fact-check log: 033 Bert Trautmann / The former paratrooper who finished a Cup final with a broken neck

## Method and limitation (read first)

WebFetch could not reach any site from the drafting environment (DNS failures and proxy refusals for mancity.com, theguardian.com, bbc.co.uk, wikipedia.org, uefa.com, rsssf.org, independent.co.uk, rogermoorhouse.com). Every check below was made against **WebSearch result excerpts** that quote the named pages, not against pages opened in full. The URLs in the story's `sources` are pages whose text was quoted in those excerpts. Wikipedia was used only as a pointer and is never counted as one of the two sources. **Before publication, an editor with full web access should open the listed URLs and confirm the wording.** Catrine Clay's book was not consulted directly; it is cited through its publisher description and reviews.
