## Giveaway audit before the reveal (48.5 s)

- Country: never named. No orange kit, no Ivory Coast flag, no Ivory Coast fans or team before the reveal (v1's celebrate_pad, drums and flag paintings removed from that part; the flag returns after the name).
- Club: no Chelsea name, crest, shirt, Stamford Bridge or Chelsea blue before the reveal. The generic dressing room's blue walls were toned to grey by a local colour edit (scene `dressing`), since all-blue read as Chelsea.
- Seasons, numbers: no season and no shirt number on screen or in the voice. The only years are 2005 (the qualification) and the owner's "between 2006 and 2010".
- Face: frame 0 and clue 3 show a backlit silhouette, painted from a real Commons photo of Drogba with every feature, colour and logo in shadow. No face before "It's Didier Drogba".
- Famous moments: no 2012 Champions League final, no Munich, no trophy with him before the reveal.
- Order: clue 1 (Africa to France at five) and clue 2 (Le Mans, contract at 21) are wide; clue 3 points to a country with a first World Cup in 2006 and a civil war; clue 4 (Golden Boot twice, four FA Cup finals) settles it just before the name.
- What still gives it away early: the hook itself. The Drogba plea is his best-known story outside the pitch, so a well-read fan may know him from frame 0. Kept on the instruction to keep the hook; an alternative is proposed to the owner in the build report.
