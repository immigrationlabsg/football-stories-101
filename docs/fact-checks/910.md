## Disputes and cautions

- **"Twice".** Goal (opened) and ESPN (search excerpt) and Wikipedia (excerpt) say Henry handled twice; Mail & Guardian and the Irish Times describe the contact without counting. Kept, as approved in final-v8. Only one of the two pages for "twice" was opened in full.
- **"The part nobody saw" / when it came out.** The payment was not disclosed until Delaney's radio interview in June 2015. A note in the research says the Irish Times wrote that the sum "surfaced in 2014", and one AP line mentions a 2014 Irish newspaper item, never found. The Reel's line is rhetorical; the page says only that Delaney "only confirmed the payment publicly in June 2015", which holds either way.
- **Dollars or euros.** FIFA's first June 2015 statement said USD 5 million; FIFA then confirmed €5 million, as the FAI and RTÉ say. Euros used throughout; the page notes the change.
- **Living persons.** Henry: the handball is an established fact that stood in the record of the match, and his replay remark is his own public statement. Delaney: named only for his own public confirmation; nothing else about him is used. Blatter: only his own public remarks. No motive or wrongdoing is alleged; "to end any claims" is how RTÉ and the Irish Times describe the agreement.
