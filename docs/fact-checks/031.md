Method note: page fetching is blocked from this session (WebFetch cannot reach any site), so every claim was checked against web-search result excerpts and the search tool's summaries of the pages named below, not by opening the full pages. Where the tool summarised several pages in one sentence without saying which page said what, the row says "per search summary" and lists the pages returned. Third pass (26 October 2026, after an independent check): the rows that leaned on mixed search summaries (the Maracanã, Pauleta's record, Messi's four Ballons d'Or) and the two weak outlets (What a Howler, Mancunian Matters) were searched again, each search restricted to one outlet's domain (allowed_domains): santosfc.com.br, espn.com.br, goal.com and aljazeera.com. Excerpts in quotation marks are wording the search tool presented as the page's own (it does not always mark verbatim text, so treat them as near-verbatim); unquoted excerpts are its summary of that outlet's pages. What a Howler and Mancunian Matters are no longer counted and were removed from the story's sources list. Pointers to deleted draft files were removed. One body change: the closing reflection is now in the present tense (see the Reflection note).

Living-persons rule: Cristiano Ronaldo, Zlatan Ibrahimović, Pauleta and Lionel Messi appear only in documented football facts (goals, a result, a record, an award). The Ballon d'Or deadline change is stated as what FIFA did; the story does not repeat the claim, made in some commentary, that the change was meant to favour Ronaldo. Pelé died in December 2022.
