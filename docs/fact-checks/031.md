Open point (third pass): ESPN Brasil's own article notes that some publications' recounts of Pelé's goals put his 1,000th on 14 November 1969, in a 3–0 win over Botafogo-PB in João Pessoa, rather than the Maracanã penalty. The story follows the count that Santos FC, ESPN Brasil's headline, History.com, El Comercio and DH Net all use (19 November 1969). The title, dek, hook and fact card 1 depend on it, so nothing was changed; if the editor wants to acknowledge the recount, a body-only clause such as "the goal counted as his 1,000th" would do it.

Resolved (fourth pass, 26 October 2026). What the outlets say, each search restricted to one domain:
- ESPN Brasil (search restricted to espn.com.br), "Há 50 anos, milésimo gol de Pelé causou polêmica e 'guerra' entre jornalistas por causa do Corinthians e do Paulistão": in 1969 A Gazeta Esportiva and A Cidade de Santos kept different counts (995 against 993 in October); in 1995 the Folha de S.Paulo argued, after three months of research, that the 1,000th came on 14 November in the friendly against Botafogo-PB in João Pessoa; even so, "a data de 19 de novembro prevaleceu como o dia da consagração do milésimo gol para todo mundo". The same outlet notes that nobody has established exactly how many goals Pelé scored (summary).
- Santos FC (search restricted to santosfc.com.br): 14 November in João Pessoa was goal 999 (a penalty in a 3–0 win), and the 1,000th was the penalty against Vasco on 19 November (summary). One Santos page dates the 1,000th differently (a 1972 goal in Chicago); it conflicts with the club's other pages and is not followed.
- Lance! (search restricted to lance.com.br): the João Pessoa match was goal 999 and the official 1,000th was the Maracanã penalty (summary). ESPN (search restricted to espn.com) and FIFA (inside.fifa.com) also give 19 November 1969 at the Maracanã, against Vasco.
- Not searchable: folha.uol.com.br and acervo.folha.com.br returned no results; globo.com, uol.com.br, estadao.com.br and bbc.com are closed to the search tool. The Folha recount is therefore known only as ESPN Brasil reports it.
Conclusion: 19 November 1969 is the accepted count everywhere checked, but the count was contested in 1969 and challenged in 1995. The body now says "It was counted as the 1,000th goal of his career" (was: "It was the 1,000th goal of his career."). The recount itself is not stated in the story (one outlet only).
