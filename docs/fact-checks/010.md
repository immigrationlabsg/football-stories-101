Checked 18 October 2026. Method note: WebFetch and curl could not open any page from
this session (network policy blocks the hosts), and BBC, Guardian, Reuters, Telegraph and
Independent are excluded from the search tool. Every claim was checked against the text of
pages **as returned in web-search result excerpts**, not against the full pages. The URLs in
`sources` are pages whose excerpts were read this way. Wikipedia appeared once and was used
only as a pointer. About 17 searches were spent on this story. No books were consulted.

**Living person and former head of state.** Politics is limited to documented electoral facts
(candidacies, results, inauguration, concession) and one documented public decision (the 2018
honours, with the documented criticism of it stated as criticism, not allegation). No
allegations of any kind are included. The story does not state that he is alive or describe
any current activity; the latest dated event is January 2024.
