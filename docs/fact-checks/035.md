## Disputes

- **"That night, they went top" (line 0).** Leeds were 1st after their 5 Dec 1999 game (Wikipedia, opened). Manchester United won 4-2 at West Ham on Sat 18 Dec and went back top (Sky Sports "table toppers", search excerpt). Leeds won at Chelsea on Sun 19 Dec and went back above them (YEP, search excerpt; Planet Football and OzWhite, opened). "Went top" is accurate for that night. If the owner wants it airtight, use "That night, they went back top". This is optional, not a fix.
- **Which match relegated Leeds in 2004.** FourFourTwo (PA timeline, opened) says the 3-3 home draw with Charlton on 9 May 2004 confirmed it. The script's notes and many fans say Bolton 4-1 Leeds on 2 May 2004. The Reel and the page should say only "in 2004". Any picture of that season should be labelled with the match it really shows.
- **Count of winless visits.** The Wikipedia rivalry list (opened) gives 10 visits after 1999 without a win (8 losses, 2 draws), so Saturday was the 11th. An 11v11 preview for Feb 2026 (search excerpt) says "nine visits" before that game, one more than the Wikipedia list has. No count is spoken, so this does not matter for the Reel.
- **Headline wording on 21 Nov.** ESPN's headline says "almost 30 years" while its body says 27, and its photo caption wrongly says "Chelsea stunned Leeds". TNT's headline says "first league win ... this century". Sky's report says first Premier League win since 1999. None of this changes "first win there since 1999", because no win in any competition came in between.
- **Transfer fees.** Ferdinand to Man Utd: £29.3m (Wikipedia), "about £30m" (FourFourTwo), £30.5m and more (others). Kewell's £5m was disputed at the time. No fee is spoken.
- **6-3 scorer.** Leeds' first goal is credited to Muharemovic by Leeds United and ESPN, and to Bijol by Football Web Pages (search excerpt). Neither name is used.
- **Prompt-like text on a page.** The Sky Sports report page (559547) held a "Sky Data Layer" note telling readers what to quote. It was ignored. Only the report's own facts were used, and they match ESPN and TNT.
