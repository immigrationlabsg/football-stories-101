# Fact-check log: 007 Leicester City / Champions at 5,000 to 1

Checked 18 October 2026. Method note: page fetching (WebFetch and curl) was blocked by the session's network policy, and BBC, Guardian, Reuters, Telegraph and Independent are excluded from the search tool. Every claim was checked against web-search excerpts from the pages named below, not by opening the full pages. About 30 searches were used. Before scheduling, an editor should open the Players' Tribune essay and an AFP copy of the Corriere della Sera interview to confirm quote wording.

Living-persons rule: every living person (Ranieri, Pearson, Vardy, Mahrez, Kanté, Lineker and others) appears only in documented football facts. The reported background to Pearson's dismissal (a matter involving his son) and an alleged eye-gouge in the Chelsea–Spurs match were deliberately left out; the club's own stated reason for the dismissal is quoted instead.
