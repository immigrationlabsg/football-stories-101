# Fact-check 009: The Maracanazo / The afternoon Uruguay silenced Rio

Checked 18 October 2026. Method note: WebFetch and curl could not open any page from
this session (network policy blocks the hosts), and BBC, Guardian, Reuters, Telegraph and
Independent are excluded from the search tool. Every claim was checked against the text of
pages **as returned in web-search result excerpts**, not against the full pages. The URLs in
`sources` are pages whose excerpts were read this way, not pages opened in full. Wikipedia
appeared once and was used only as a pointer. About 17 searches were spent on this story
(three were refused for blocked domains and re-run). Anything not supported by two sources
this way was cut or softened. No books were consulted.
