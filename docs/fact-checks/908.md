## Disputes

- **Mother and sister, or sister only.** Zidane: mother and sister, repeated. Materazzi: his sister only, never his mother. The Reel and the story give both, each attributed, and do not choose.
- **Materazzi's own account moved.** July 2006: admitted an insult without details, and RTE reports him rejecting the worst claims, including about Zidane's sister. September 2006 and 2016: says it concerned the sister. We use his September 2006 words and say nothing about the earlier denial.
- **Which sister line.** His later autobiography gives a coarser version. Only the 2006 printable line is used.
- **Who provoked whom.** Zidane: "the guilty party is the one who provokes", meaning Materazzi. Materazzi: "I did not cause it", and he calls Zidane's shirt remark the provocation. Neither is in the Reel or the story now; both stories of the exchange are told, each attributed.
- **Regret.** 2006: "I don't regret my behaviour" (regretting would mean Materazzi was right). 2010: "Of course I reproach myself", but no apology to Materazzi. The Reel uses only the refusal; the story dates all three.
- **Golden Ball vote.** Reports differ on when voting closed: mostly at half-time (BBC Sport, opened; also a search excerpt of an AP report on Southeast Missourian, which blocked opening), or midnight Sunday (a search excerpt). So the Reel says nothing about timing, and the story gives half-time as the BBC's report, not as fact. Al Jazeera, 12 Jul 2006: Blatter hinted he might lose it. No report found that he did, and he is listed as winner today (Wikipedia).
- **Age error.** FilGoal gives him as 35; he was 34 (Mail & Guardian). Not used.

The lip-reader and "terrorist" claims (Al Jazeera, 12 Jul 2006; Materazzi denied them: RTE, Mail & Guardian; FIFA reported no racist content). "To protect his sister's dignity" as a fact about his mind: he said what drove him, and the Reel reports that. Walking past the trophy: two searches found no source to open.
