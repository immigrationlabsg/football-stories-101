# Fact-check log: 040 Riquelme v Ronaldinho / Two number 10s on 28 November, and one Barcelona shirt

Slot: Saturday 28 November 2026 ("On this day"). Checked 26 October 2026; sourcing re-checked the same day (third pass, see below).

Method note: page fetching was blocked (WebFetch could not resolve hosts from this session), so every claim was checked against WebSearch result excerpts from the pages named below, not by opening the full pages. Several key sources are Spanish-language Argentine outlets (El Gráfico, Cadena 3, 442 Perfil, Goal Argentina, Diario de Cuyo, La Nación). Where the search summary did not say which of two pages carried a line, both are named and the row is marked "(search summary)". Before scheduling, an editor should open RSSSF's 2000 Intercontinental Cup and 2005 Ballon d'Or pages, UEFA's "Riquelme takes Villarreal option" and "Villarreal send Riquelme to Boca", and 442 Perfil's Figo shirt article.

Third pass (26 October 2026, after an independent check): Wikipedia is no longer one of the two counted sources in any row; it stays in this log only as a pointer, and its six URLs were removed from the story's sources list. Each new search in this pass was restricted to one outlet's domain (allowed_domains), and the row says so ("search restricted to uefa.com"), so the excerpt is attributable to that outlet even where the tool did not say which of its pages carried the line. Excerpts in quotation marks are wording the search tool presented as the page's own (it does not always mark verbatim text, so treat them as near-verbatim); unquoted excerpts are its summary of that outlet's pages. One body change: the Copa Libertadores final sentence (see that row and "Changed in the third pass").

Living-persons rule: Riquelme, Ronaldinho, Palermo, Roberto Carlos, Makélélé, Lampard, Gerrard, Van Gaal, Lehmann and Figo are all alive and appear only in documented football facts. Left out on purpose: Ronaldinho's 2020 detention in Paraguay, Riquelme's role and disputes as Boca Juniors president, and the remark sometimes attributed to Van Gaal that Riquelme was a "political signing" (the wording is attributed differently across sources).

Story bank: the reserved angle on Ronaldinho (the night the Bernabéu stood and applauded a Barcelona player, 19 November 2005) is reserved in docs/story-bank.md and is not used or mentioned here. The 19 November "On this day" slot went to Pelé v Cristiano Ronaldo (031), which does not use that night, so there is no Ronaldinho clash nine days apart.
