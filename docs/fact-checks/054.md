## Legendary XI (lineup)

England starting XI, 6 October 2001: Martyn; Gary Neville, Ferdinand, Keown, Ashley Cole; Beckham (captain), Gerrard, Scholes, Barmby; Fowler, Heskey.

- Source 1, BBC (opened): "England: Martyn, Gary Neville, Ferdinand, Keown, Ashley Cole, Beckham, Gerrard, Scholes, Barmby, Fowler, Heskey." Subs: Southgate, Wright, Carragher, Murphy, McManaman, Sheringham, Andy Cole.
- Source 2, 11v11 (opened): Starting lineup Martyn, Ashley Cole, Ferdinand, Keown, Gary Neville, Beckham (captain), Gerrard, Scholes, Barmby, Fowler, Heskey. Subs: Andrew Cole for Barmby 46, Sheringham for Fowler 68, McManaman for Ashley Cole 78.
- Check, SM (opened): "ENGLAND: Martyn; G Neville, Ferdinand, Keown, Ashley Cole (McManaman); Beckham, Gerrard, Scholes, Barmby (Andy Cole); Fowler (Sheringham), Heskey". Eleven names, all three agree. The "Cole" in row three is Ashley Cole; Andy Cole came on at half-time and did not start.

Layout: the BBC and SM team sheets list each line right to left (Neville first, Cole last; Beckham first, Barmby last), so the rows were reversed to left to right (Barmby, Scholes, Gerrard, Beckham; Cole, Keown, Ferdinand, Neville; Heskey, Fowler). No source gives a diagram, so the order inside the centre-back pair, the central-midfield pair and the strike pair is the team-sheet convention, not a documented fact. A human may wish to look at that. "Cole" alone on the card could be read as Andy Cole; the surname is the card convention, so left as is.
