# Fact-check: 023 Wimbledon / Two penalty saves, 13 years apart, from the Southern League to the FA Cup

## Method and limitation (read first)

WebFetch could not reach any site from the drafting environment. All checks were made against **WebSearch result excerpts** that quote the named pages, not against pages opened in full. Wikipedia was used only as a pointer and is never counted as one of the two sources. **An editor with full web access should open the listed URLs before publication.**

Living-persons rule: Guy, Bassett, Beasant, Sanchez, Wise, Fashanu, Gould, Aldridge and Goodyear are named for documented football events only. There is no conduct material of any kind. The FourFourTwo headline's anecdote, the Princess Diana detail and all dressing-room stories were deliberately left out.
