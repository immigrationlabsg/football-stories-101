## Disputes and cautions

- **When FIFA moved the deadline.** FourFourTwo and Fox reported it on 20 Nov 2013, and Wikipedia gives 20 Nov as the announcement date. ESPN's Graham Hunter says voters got FIFA's email on Tue 19 Nov, while the match was still 0–0. Neither the script nor the page names a day. The hook says "should have closed", not "had closed".
- **Why it moved.** Only FIFA's stated reason (low response) is used, as reported speech. Commentary that the change was meant to help Ronaldo is opinion about a living person and is **not used**, as in the 057 log.
- **Living persons.** Ronaldo, Ibrahimović and Messi appear only in documented football facts: goals, a result and an award.
