## Suspected problems found on an editorial read (each still needs sources)

- **1971 line-up paragraph**: Ruud Krol is believed to have **missed the 1971 final injured**, and Neeskens is thought to have played at right-back. Arie Haan may have come on as a substitute. As written, the "side Michels took to Wembley" paragraph is probably wrong. Check it against the official line-up.
- The 1966 fog match was against Liverpool in the second round. Verify, or remove, the claim that Shankly walked onto the pitch (the brief flags it as doubtful).
- "Football is war": check the original Dutch wording, the date and that Michels said it.
- Hulshoff quote: verify that it appears in *Brilliant Orange* with this wording, or paraphrase it.
- Cruyff quote ("Playing football is very simple..."): find where it was first recorded.
- Kovács and Steaua: did he win the league title, or only cups?
- 1972 final: the description of Cruyff's two goals and their minutes. Timeline says "League, cup and Intercontinental Cup follow", but the league and cup were won by May 1972, so they did not follow the final.
- The 1972/73 Super Cup against Rangers was unofficial in UEFA's records (Rangers were banned at the time). Say so.
- 1973 final: the minute of Rep's header (usually given as the 4th, not "after five minutes").
- "The one that won it four times in the next two decades": Barcelona won in 2006, 2009, 2011 and 2015, which runs beyond 20 years from 1992. Reword.
- Michels appointed January 1965, aged 36. Check that Ajax finished 13th of 16 and that he was a PE teacher.
- Captaincy vote 1973 and the fee (6 million guilders, about £922,000, a world record).
- Deaths: Michels 2005, Kovács 1995, Cruyff March 2016, Neeskens 2024. **Living persons**: "Krol, Haan, Swart and Rep still turn up at the stadium" claims all four are alive and attend, without a source. Verify each or cut.
- "Cruyff and Keizer ... never fully repaired the breach": no source given; check or cut.
