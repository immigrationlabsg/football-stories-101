## Suspected problems found on an editorial read (each still needs sources)

- **Misattributed quote**: "I'm not equipped to manage successfully without ... I am the shop
  window and he's the goods in the back" is widely attributed to **Clough about Taylor**, not to Taylor.
  The draft gives it to Peter Taylor, mentions "Brian Clough" inside it and has no source. Verify, or cut it.
- **Chronology**: Clough went to Brighton (November 1973 to July 1974) **before** his 44 days at Leeds.
  The draft reverses the order.
- Kenny Burns probably joined in **July 1977, after promotion**, but the draft puts him in the promotion season. Larry Lloyd's arrival date and fee from Coventry also need checking.
- Shilton's fee (often given as £270,000 rather than £250,000) and the month he arrived. Also check "18 goals in 37 games".
- The 42-match unbeaten run probably began after the defeat at Leeds on 19 November 1977, with the first game on 26 November. Check both end dates.
- Bolton v Wolves, Mallorca and "heard in a bar": check every element, or cut.
- The League Cup replay (Old Trafford, Robertson penalty) and Chris Woods's age and whether he had played any league games.
- Cologne semi-final: first-leg sequence (2–0 down, 3–2 up, Okudera equaliser). Check the minute of Bowyer's header (65th?).
- The £999,999 story: what reason Clough gave, and the final cost.
- "His header ... was the only time he touched the ball in the Malmö penalty area": unverifiable, **cut** (the brief already says so).
- "Tumbled into the shot-put circle" and "a city of 280,000 people": check or cut.
- When and where Francis was injured (Achilles, v Crystal Palace?), how long before the final, and the 20th-minute Robertson goal in Madrid.
- The Clough quote about Robertson ("Picasso of our game"): check that it is in the 1994 autobiography with that wording.
- Taylor and Clough falling out, Taylor signing Robertson for Derby (1983), Taylor's death (October 1990), Clough's death (September 2004), the A52 "Brian Clough Way" and Francis's death (July 2023).
- **Living persons**: "Robertson, Shilton, Birtles, Burns and Anderson are still regularly asked..." makes an implicit claim that all are alive, and that claim has no source. Verify each, or cut the sentence.
