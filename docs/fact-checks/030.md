## Disputes

- **The hour.** 1am (Pochettino, FourFourTwo and Sportwitness), 1.30 (442 Perfil), 2am (Griffa via Diario de Cuyo; gate3-v2 also notes a FourFourTwo 2am line). We give his own 1am, plainly.
- **Who opened the door.** Mother (FourFourTwo) or grandmother Rosa (442 Perfil, per Amalia). Reel and story say "his family".
- **Who praised the legs.** Bielsa (FourFourTwo; Pochettino's telling) or Griffa (Mail on Sunday, his own account). Reel and story say "one of them".
- **How many minutes.** Five (Sky Sports) or three (FourFourTwo). "Within minutes".
- **Start date.** Club statement: from 18 Nov. Search results: USA v Haiti on 18 Nov, takes charge on Thursday. "This week" holds.
