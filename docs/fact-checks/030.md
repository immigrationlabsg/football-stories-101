## Disputes and notes
- **Time of the opener on Tuesday.** AJ2: ninth minute. Yahoo (CBF): eighth minute. Neither the Reel nor the page gives the minute; "12 minutes" is AJ2's figure and is also in KHEL.
- **Seventh goal.** Allan (AJ2, Yahoo) or own goal by Raoul Suhaimi (Khel Now). Page credits Allan with the majority.
- **"Called inexperienced".** Ancelotti also said "it's not his fault". The Reel wording was approved; the page gives both halves.
- **Referee decisions.** Only Brazil's side says Foo erred; the page attributes every complaint to Brazil, Ancelotti or Raphinha and states that VAR upheld the goal (living-persons rule).
- **Not used (one source only):** Brazil's biggest win since 8–0 v China in 2012 (Yahoo only); Foo's church-group start (ActiveSG only); Foo's Asian Games and Ronaldo games (ActiveSG only).
