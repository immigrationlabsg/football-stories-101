## Rules for new angles
- **Never repeat a story.** A subject may come back only with a new angle the earlier story did not cover, marked
  "(follow-up of NNN)" here and `followUpOf: NNN` in the story's front matter, and it may repeat at most a one-line
  recap of the earlier story. Example: 055, Ronaldo's second career, follows 001 (the knees and the 2002 World Cup).
  People may appear in more than one story (Beckham in the Class of 92 and in his own story) as long as each story
  tells a different event; never two stories about the same match or tournament within a week.
- **Spread the world through stars fans know:** African, Asian and North American stories are told through players
  with a European or world profile (Okocha, Drogba, Mané, Eto'o, Weah, Son and the like), never through clubs or
  leagues most fans have no context for. Women's football at least every other week, again through names and
  moments with real reach.
- **Lead with names fans know.** At least half of each week's stories are built around globally famous players or teams
  (Zlatan, Ronaldinho, Rivaldo, Romário, Beckham and the like). Their documented careers are fair subjects while they are alive.
  The promise stays the same: a true, sourced story that makes a fan say "I never knew that", not news or gossip.
- **"On this day" means a game or a star (owner, 25 Oct 2026).** An anniversary story is always a match that was played,
  or something that happened to a football star on that date. Never a founding, a meeting, a governing body or a rule
  change (no "the FA was founded" stories). If no star or famous match fits a date, skip the anniversary that day.
- **Keep the rotation:** player, club, era, moment, in that order, with no more than two stories in a row from one country. The "on this day"
  slots (every third day) sit outside the rotation; the other stories keep it around them.
- **Use the research notes** in `docs/research/` for ideas and timing, but only as inspiration; never copy another account's post.
