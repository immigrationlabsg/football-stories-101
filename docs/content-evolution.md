# Content evolution: ideas for the owner to review later

Nothing here is in use. The page stays on its original promise until the owner agrees to a change.

- **Recent stories with history behind them:** when something happens today (a record, a farewell, a final), tell the
  older story that explains it. Example: a club's first title in decades, told through the last time.
- **Short series:** three or four linked stories, for example "Brazil's three Rs" (Ronaldo, Rivaldo, Ronaldinho), or
  "Before they were famous".
- **On-this-day posts:** match the date of a story to the anniversary of the event.
- **Reels:** a 20 to 30 second version of the carousel, with the cards as slides and captions on screen.
- **Reader questions:** once there is an audience, answer the best reply to the closing question with a story.
