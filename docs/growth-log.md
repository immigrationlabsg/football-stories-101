# Growth log

What we changed to grow the page, why, and what the numbers did afterwards. The weekly review (Mondays) adds a section
here: the **Audience report** figures for the week, what worked, what didn't, and the next experiment. One change at a
time where possible, so each result can be traced to its cause.

## The owner's brief (27 Oct 2026)

"Be the greatest influencer in the world: grow the popularity and reach of this account, and bring great football
history, stories and comparisons to more football fanatics and readers in the world. Keep wowing us, keep our attention,
and bring us these amazing stories every day." Growth is our job, not the owner's: no asking friends to follow, no
engagement bait; reach comes from stories worth sending to a friend, strong first seconds, and learning from the
numbers every week. The owner does not want to be reminded about the Facebook password / exposed keys (decided 27 Oct).

## Baseline, 27 Oct 2026 (first 8 days, 20-27 Oct)

| | Followers | Picture posts (20-24 Oct) | Reels (25-27 Oct) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 0 | 0-1 accounts reached per post | 106, 108, 122 accounts reached per post |
| Threads | 0 | 19-70 views per post (avg 34) | 23, 0, 11 views per post |
| Facebook | 0 | 1-9 views per post | 5, 1, 1 plays per Reel (avg watch 5.5 s, 1.8 s, 4.6 s) |

- Instagram, last 28 days: reach 340, views 371, 5 interactions (5 likes; no comments, saves or shares), 6 profile visits.
- Threads, total: 283 views, no likes, replies or reposts.
- Facebook: readable since the new Page key of 27 Oct (with `read_insights`). Facebook barely distributes us yet: single-
  digit plays per Reel against ~110 accounts per Reel on Instagram, and viewers leave within 2-6 seconds of a ~40-second
  Reel. "People reached" (post_impressions_unique) is no longer offered by Meta for Reels; the weekly review should find
  its replacement metric.
- *Biggest lesson so far:* Instagram is where the reach is. On Facebook the first seconds are not holding anyone yet,
  which is exactly what the new cover lines (from 29 Oct) target: watch the average watch time.

**What went well:** switching Instagram to Reels on 25 Oct took reach per post from about 1 to about 110, all of it from
non-followers (we have none). **What did not:** nobody has interacted yet beyond 5 likes; Threads fell after the switch.

## Changes on 27 Oct 2026 (owner approved items 4, 5 and 7)

1. **Threads leads with the cards.** Threads drew more views with the card carousel than with the Reel, so the post is now
   the teaser + the 7 cards + the topic tag "Football"; the Reel and the Instagram link go in a reply. *Watch:* Threads
   views per post from 28 Oct against the 34 average for cards and 11 for Reels.
2. **Sharper first card.** The first 2-3 seconds decide whether Meta shows a Reel to more people. The comparison covers
   now open on the surprise ("An 18-year-old sub beat Barcelona. Two years earlier to the day, Torres floored Chelsea.")
   instead of "On this day, ...", and the quiz covers carry their first clue ("He grew up in a refugee hotel. Can you name
   him in 4 clues?") instead of "4 clues. How many do you need?". Regular stories already opened on the surprise.
   *Watch:* Instagram reach and Facebook plays for comparisons and quizzes against regular stories.
3. **No "send this to a mate" line.** Item 6 was approved but not done: Meta has demoted posts that ask for likes, shares
   or tags ("engagement bait") since 2017, which would cost reach. Shares have to come from the story itself.
4. **Weekly review** every Monday (see top).
