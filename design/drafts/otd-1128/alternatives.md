# 28 November: runner-up pairs

- **Best v Chilavert / Two unlikely scorers on 28 November**: George Best scored on his Stockport County debut, a 3–2 home win over Swansea City at Edgeley Park in the Fourth Division, 28 Nov 1975 (QFax "On this day 28 November"; Swansea City, "Andy Leitch: sharing a pitch with George Best"; Wikipedia George Best). José Luis Chilavert, Paraguay's goalkeeper, scored a hat-trick, all penalties, in Vélez Sarsfield's 6–1 win over Ferro Carril Oeste, 28 Nov 1999 (Guinness World Records "most goals scored by a goalkeeper in one game"; Planet Football; Billiken). Caveats: clashes with the 25 Nov Best v Maradona story if both run; one report dates Chilavert's game 29 Nov (most say 28); one source says Best set up two and scored the third, while Wikipedia credits him with 2 goals in 3 Stockport games, so the debut goal needs a match report.
- **Riquelme v Chilavert / Two nights in Argentina's football on 28 November**: Riquelme's Tokyo masterclass, Boca 2–1 Real Madrid, 28 Nov 2000 (Wikipedia "2000 Intercontinental Cup", RSSSF, El Gráfico) against Chilavert's penalty hat-trick, 28 Nov 1999 (sources above). Lower global reach than Ronaldinho; use only if Ronaldinho clashes with the 19 Nov slot.
