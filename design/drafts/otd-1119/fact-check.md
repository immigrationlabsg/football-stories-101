# Fact-check log: NNN Pelé v Cristiano Ronaldo / A 1,000th goal, a hat-trick in Sweden and one night in Zurich

Slot: Thursday 19 November 2026 ("On this day" comparison). Not a story-bank angle. Rewritten 26 October 2026 from the earlier Pelé v Ronaldinho draft, because Ronaldinho and his 2005 Ballon d'Or are already the second half of the 28 November comparison (Riquelme v Ronaldinho). The Ronaldinho Bernabéu material is kept in alternatives.md for a later regular story.

Checked 26 October 2026. Method note: page fetching is blocked from this session (WebFetch cannot reach any site), so every claim was checked against web-search result excerpts and the search tool's summaries of the pages named below, not by opening the full pages. Searches were restricted to named outlets (allowed domains) so that each excerpt can be attributed to one outlet. Where the tool summarised several pages in one sentence without saying which page said what, the row says "per search summary" and lists the pages returned. The Pelé rows marked "kept" were confirmed in the earlier version of this log (same date) and are carried over unchanged, except where a new source is added. Two rows rest on rows already confirmed in this repository's own logs (docs/fact-checks/010.md and 014.md); their sources are named as logged there. Before scheduling, an editor should open the CNN pages of 19 November 2013 and 20 November 2013 and the ESPN Ballon d'Or voting piece.

Living-persons rule: Cristiano Ronaldo, Zlatan Ibrahimović, Pauleta and Lionel Messi appear only in documented football facts (goals, a result, a record, an award). The Ballon d'Or deadline change is stated as what FIFA did; the story does not repeat the claim, made in some commentary, that the change was meant to favour Ronaldo. Pelé died in December 2022.
