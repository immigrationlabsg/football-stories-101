# 22 November: runner-up pairs

1. **Messi v Giroud, "two scoring records on 22 November"**: Lionel Messi's hat-trick in Barcelona's 5–1 win over Sevilla at the Camp Nou, 22 Nov 2014, took him to 253 La Liga goals, past Telmo Zarra's 251 that had stood since 1955 (free kick in the 21st minute to draw level, then 72' and 78'). Key sources: ESPN FC "Barcelona's Lionel Messi breaks La Liga goalscoring record vs Sevilla"; Al Jazeera "Messi becomes La Liga's highest scorer" (22 Nov 2014); Capital FM Kenya; LaLiga.com "252 goals and a place in history" (already logged in docs/fact-checks/011.md; note FC Barcelona gives Zarra 252). Twist: on the day Giroud matched Henry, Messi's Argentina lost 2–1 to Saudi Arabia, ending a 36-match unbeaten run (Ahram/AFP "Saudi Arabia stun world with 2-1 victory over Messi's Argentina"; NBC Bay Area). Uses Messi, so only if 29 Oct's Messi v Ronaldo does not crowd it.
2. **Messi v Del Piero, "the first goal and the record goal"**: Alessandro Del Piero's first professional goal, aged 18, for Padova against Ternana in Serie B, 22 Nov 1992 (TuttoMercatoWeb "Accadde oggi: 22 novembre 1992"; Sky Sport Italia Del Piero shirts gallery; Lazio Stories season table, 10 games and 1 goal in 1992–93). Final score 5–0 per TMW and Forza Italian Football (the latter misplaces the season), so check the score before use; pair with Messi's 2014 record above.

Checked and rejected: Saudi Arabia 2–1 Argentina alone (a team shock, and Messi again); the 22 Nov 1997 Milan derby, 2–2 with goals from Ronaldo and George Weah (one match, not two moments; Weah has story 010); Honvéd in Bilbao, 22 Nov 1956 (already the opening of story 002, Puskás).
