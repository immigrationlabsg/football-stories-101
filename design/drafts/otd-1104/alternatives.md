# 4 November: pairs considered

**Used (26 Oct): Owen v Beckham, the same afternoon at Anfield, 4 November 2001.** Owen scored twice and Beckham scored United's goal in Liverpool 3–1 Manchester United. The turn is the two December 2001 votes: Owen won the Ballon d'Or (176 points, Beckham fourth with 102), while in FIFA's coaches' vote Beckham had the most first-place votes (30 to Figo's 27) and still lost by 12 points. It closes on Owen being given United's number 7 in 2009. Fully sourced; see fact-check.md. Beckham's Greece free kick (6 Oct 2001, reserved in docs/story-bank.md, number 040 as of 26 Oct) is not told.
