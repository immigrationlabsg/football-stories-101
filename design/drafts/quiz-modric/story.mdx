Luka Modrić was a boy when war reached his home in Croatia. His family became refugees and lived in the Hotel Kolovare in Zadar.

He and his friends were rarely seen without a ball.

In 2003, aged 18, Dinamo Zagreb loaned him to Zrinjski Mostar in Bosnia and Herzegovina. He was voted the league's player of the year.

At the 2018 World Cup he led Croatia to their first final, which they lost to France. He won the Golden Ball as the best player of the tournament.

That December he won the Ballon d'Or. Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo had shared it for the ten years before; the last winner outside the two had been Kaká, in 2007.

Here's what stays with me.

A boy who learned the game around a refugee hotel ended a decade of Messi and Ronaldo.
